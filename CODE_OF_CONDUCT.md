# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AgentForge a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints,
accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, sexualized attention, trolling, insults, threats, publishing private
information, or conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit,
remove, or reject comments, commits, code, Issues, and other contributions that do not align with
this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents AgentForge in
public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact channel. If the
report itself contains sensitive security information, use the private advisory channel described
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy as far as practical and
apply consequences proportionate to impact and history, from a private correction through a
temporary or permanent ban.
